# 🥐 Bakery Product Performance Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
Bakery Product Performance Analysis is a business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze bakery sales data. The dashboard tracks key performance indicators (KPIs), product performance, and sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze total revenue, transactions, and quantity sold.
- Identify top-performing bakery products by revenue and quantity.
- Understand sales patterns by hour, month, and day of the week.
- Present business insights and actionable recommendations through an executive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **CSV Dataset** — Bakery sales data
- **DAX** — KPI calculations and measures
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **Git & GitHub** — Version control and project documentation

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Transaction Value
- Unique Products
- Average Selling Price

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations
1. Top 10 Bakery Products by Revenue
2. Revenue by Hour
3. Revenue by Month
4. Transactions by Hour
5. Quantity Sold by Product
6. Revenue by Day of the Week

## 🔍 Key Findings
- **Top Revenue-Generating Product:** Traditional Baguette, with €144,756.05 in revenue.
- **Peak Sales Hour:** 11:00 AM, with €111,557.86 in revenue.
- **Best-Performing Month:** August, generating €102,538.00 in revenue.
- **Peak Transaction Hour:** 11:00 AM, with 28,793 transactions.
- **Highest Quantity Sold:** Traditional Baguette, with 117,463 units.
- **Best-Performing Day:** Sunday, generating €126,339.88 in revenue.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Monitor Traditional Baguette inventory to support its strong sales performance.
- Plan staffing and product availability around peak sales hours.
- Investigate the lower revenue observed in November and on Wednesdays.
- Review low-performing products to understand their sales patterns and potential improvement opportunities.
- Validate late-evening activity against business operating hours and available records before making operational changes.

4. Explore the KPI cards, visualizations, and business insights.

## 📌 Project Scope
This project focuses on descriptive sales analytics and business intelligence. It uses historical sales records to summarize performance and identify patterns. The analysis highlights associations and comparisons; it does not establish the causes of sales differences.
